## Project overview

Rawand provides printers, scanners, copiers, plotters, inks, and related supplies in Saudi Arabia.

The project turned the company website into a more useful online communication hub, combining product discovery, educational content, promotions, and a clearer route to purchase or contact the team.

## Key features

The website gives product categories, useful publishing tools, and conversion paths equal weight so the business can sell and educate from the same platform.
