## Project overview

Tungsten Media needed a portfolio website that could make its range of digital services and completed work easy to understand at a glance.

We shaped the experience around the work itself, giving services, selected projects, and contact pathways a clear hierarchy that supports both discovery and decision-making.

## Project approach

The site combines a confident visual presentation with a practical content structure. Responsive layouts, focused project storytelling, and direct calls to action help visitors move from understanding the studio to starting a conversation.
