## Project overview

Najmat Al Diwan is a Saudi construction and contracting company with services spanning construction, renovation, interior design, electrical installations, and plumbing.

The website was created to strengthen the company’s position, communicate its capabilities, showcase completed work, and make it easier for potential clients to inquire.

## Project approach

The site puts service clarity, project proof, testimonials, and contact access in a straightforward structure that supports both trust and lead generation.
