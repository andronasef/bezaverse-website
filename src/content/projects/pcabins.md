## Project overview

Portable House Factory designs and manufactures prefabricated houses, cabins, and mobile homes for residential and commercial applications.

The website was developed to strengthen the brand, present customizable solutions clearly, showcase completed work, and make it easier for potential customers to ask questions or request a quote.

## Key features

The project brings product discovery, service information, proof of quality, and direct inquiry into one bilingual experience.
