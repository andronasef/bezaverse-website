## Project overview

The Mustard Seed Project works to transform lives in Upper Egypt through free, high-quality education and comprehensive support for underprivileged children and their mothers.

The website brings the organization’s mission, programs, impact, and ways to support into a clear online presence for donors, volunteers, partners, and families.

## Project approach

The implementation translates the visual and interactive direction into a live website with distinct program areas, impact storytelling, supporter pathways, and accessible contact options.
