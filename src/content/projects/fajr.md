## Project overview

FAJR sits at the intersection of Egyptian heritage and modern craftsmanship, creating premium handcrafted leather goods for contemporary life.

The ecommerce experience acts as a digital flagship for the brand, putting product quality, storytelling, community, and a frictionless purchase journey in the same frame.

## Project approach

The experience uses a restrained visual system, focused product presentation, community proof, and a clear path from discovery to checkout to make the heritage-led story work as a modern storefront.
