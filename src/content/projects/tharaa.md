## Project overview

Tharaa is a luxury abaya brand with a focus on premium presentation, seasonal offers, and an effortless shopping experience.

The store gives customers a structured catalogue, clearer product information, promotional moments, and a checkout journey designed to make purchase decisions easier.

## Project approach

The experience balances visual elegance with the practical details that help an ecommerce visitor decide: useful categories, product context, offers, trust signals, and fast support.
