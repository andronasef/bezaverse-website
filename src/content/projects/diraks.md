## Project overview

Diraks delivers fresh, high-quality fish from Jazan’s waters, with a focus on authenticity, premium packaging, and same-day delivery.

The store reflects that identity while making it easier for customers to browse products, understand the offer, and place an order quickly.

## Project approach

The design connects a strong local story with ecommerce fundamentals: fast discovery, clear delivery expectations, direct support, and payment flexibility.
