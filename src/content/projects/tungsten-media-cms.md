## Project overview

Tungsten Media needed more than a marketing website: the team needed a practical system for owning and maintaining the content behind it.

We designed and developed a custom content management platform alongside the public-facing site. It gives administrators a single place to manage pages, visual sections, service content, users, roles, bookings, fixed materials, social links, and payment gateways.

## Project approach

The platform was shaped around everyday content operations. Clear navigation, focused forms, and structured permissions make complex site administration approachable, while Arabic and English support keeps the experience useful for the whole team.
