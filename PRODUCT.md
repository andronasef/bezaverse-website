# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Prospective agency clients evaluating whether an experienced digital product and web team can understand their business, show credible work, and make it easy to start a conversation. They may arrive from a referral on desktop or return on a phone.

## Product Purpose

Present a clear, image-led agency story across Home, About, Projects, and Contact. The site should quickly explain the offer, build confidence through proof, and convert interest into a project conversation. The first release uses six2eight as a structural and imagery reference, while the brand layer is deliberately replaceable.

## Brand Personality

Confident, human, precise. The voice is direct and reassuring, with enough energy to feel like a creative partner rather than a procurement form.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic SaaS dashboards, repeated icon-card grids, timid beige minimalism, noisy gradients, and a homepage that hides the contact path. Avoid hard-coding the reference agency identity into the layout system.

## Design Principles

1. Show the work early and make it legible.
2. Let spacing and typography create hierarchy before adding containers.
3. Make every route lead naturally toward a conversation.
4. Keep imagery and copy editable so the future brand can replace them cleanly.
5. Motion should guide attention, never delay understanding.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast and keyboard operation. Preserve visible focus, minimum touch targets, semantic landmarks, reduced-motion behavior, readable line lengths, and clear form validation.
